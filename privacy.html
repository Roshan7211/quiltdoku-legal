Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What Quiltdoku collects

Quiltdoku itself does not collect, send or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and nothing you do in the game leaves your device. On Android the app shows no ads and has no internet access at all.

Ads (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, Quiltdoku shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between puzzles, and a short video you can choose to watch for an extra hint. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's IP address (which can show your approximate location), your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), which ads you see and tap, and app performance and crash data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, under Ad choices. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Quiltdoku itself never sees or keeps it.

What stays on your phone

Your quilts, streaks, best times, settings and any puzzle in progress are saved on your device only, so you can pick up where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them. If your phone backs up app data, they are included in that backup, which is yours.

Sharing

When you share a result or save a quilt as a wallpaper, the app makes a picture (and, for a result, a line of text) and hands it to your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app sends nothing itself and asks for no access to your photos.

Ratings

Now and then the app asks the App Store or Google Play to show its own rating box. Any rating or review you give goes to the store, under your store account; the app never sees it.

Your choices and deleting your data

Quiltdoku keeps nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to hold or delete: your game data exists only on your device, and deleting the app removes it. To withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time, use Ad choices in the game's Settings (where shown), or switch off tracking for Quiltdoku in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. Information Google collects to show ads is kept and deleted under Google's own policy; you can review and manage it at myadcenter.google.com and policies.google.com/privacy. Questions or requests: infotech@hexavo.co.uk.

Children

Quiltdoku is not directed at children, and Quiltdoku itself collects no information from anyone, including children.
