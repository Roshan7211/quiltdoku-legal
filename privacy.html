Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

No data collected

Quiltdoku does not collect, send or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no internet access at all.

What stays on your phone

Your quilts, streaks, best times, settings and any puzzle in progress are saved on your device only, so you can pick up where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them. If your phone backs up app data, they are included in that backup, which is yours.

Sharing

When you share a result or save a quilt as a wallpaper, the app makes a picture (and, for a result, a line of text) and hands it to your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app sends nothing itself and asks for no access to your photos.

Ratings

Now and then the app asks the App Store or Google Play to show its own rating box. Any rating or review you give goes to the store, under your store account; the app never sees it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
