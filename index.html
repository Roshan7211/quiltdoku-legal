Quiltdoku support

Quiltdoku is a cozy logic puzzle: cut the board into fabric patches, and every puzzle you solve is sewn into a quilt. There is a new daily puzzle every day, unlimited puzzles from 5×5 to 10×10, and a three-minute speed round. It works entirely on your device — no account, no ads, no tracking.

Contact

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? E-mail infotech@hexavo.co.uk and include your phone model and iOS version.

How to play

Cut the board into rectangular patches. Every patch holds exactly one button, and the button says what its patch must be:

  • A number — the patch covers that many squares.
  • A square button — the patch is a square, any size.
  • A wide button — the patch is wider than it is tall.
  • A tall button — the patch is taller than it is wide.
  • Drag across the board to lay a patch; tap a patch to unpick it.
  • Lay a patch over another and the old one is trimmed, not lost.
  • Stuck? A hint lights up a button, then shows its patch, then places it.
  • Every puzzle has one answer, and you never need to guess.

The tutorial and these rules are always in Settings.

Questions

When does the daily puzzle change? At midnight in your phone's own time zone. Everyone gets the same puzzle that day.

Can I hide the clock? Yes: turn on Zen mode in Settings. No times are recorded against you.

Can I turn off the music or sounds? Yes, separately, in Settings. Music also stays quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on.

I left a puzzle half done. It is kept: the home screen shows Resume.

Your data

Your quilts, streaks, best times and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
